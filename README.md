# haven-hk-game
Theme: Bounce Back
"Potentially" is a cozy physics based platformer about a small cube called Gary bouncing around. 20 minutes before you got the game, he somehow bound a comically large spring to himself. With his newfound comically large spring, he now bounces around an unnamed city. He has no goal, and he must bounce (back) somewhere.

(The game is based around conservation of energy, where you are given a fixed amount of energy to go through the environment, with additional variables that can affect your future momentum.)

​Made for Hack Club Haven 2026.
